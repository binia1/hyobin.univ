International Admissions

Admission Guidelines for International Students (2025)

1

Eligibility

Parents and students must both be non-Korean nationals.
High school graduates (or expected).

2

Language Proficiency

TOPIK Level 3 or higher is required.
(Department of Filipino Language requires English proficiency only)

3

Scholarship

HBNU Global Scholarship: 30% ~ 100% tuition waiver based on GPA and TOPIK score.
* Special scholarship for students from sister cities (e.g., Numazu).
